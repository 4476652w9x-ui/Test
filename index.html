Assumptions & key findings
  • Scenarios. A — relocate to Queenstown NZ (12 mo). B — travel year: Mexico (3) → Canada (2) → France (3) → Georgia (4). C — Queenstown NZ (10 mo) + Chamonix peak ski Jan–Feb (2 mo). D — Melbourne base (10 mo) + Chamonix Jan–Feb (2 mo).
  • Chamonix is the biggest seasonal risk. Jan–Feb peak ski accommodation runs ~AUD $6.5k–10k/mo (point ~$8k) — roughly double general France. It is a separate line, not inherited from France.
  • Schooling ≈ $0. Assuming Australian/NZ citizenship, the child is a domestic student — NZ & Melbourne public school is free (small voluntary donations only). Short Chamonix & travel-year stints use distance education (materials only).
  • NZ car = depreciation, not cash. Modelled as ~$6k net depreciation over 12 mo (resold on exit), plus ~$4.5k removal/shipping — not a $30k cash outlay.
  • Mounjaro. $800/mo covers low–mid dose; raise toward $1,000 for a high maintenance dose. For the travel year the real risk is continuity of supply — a pre-departure stockpile is modelled as a one-off.
  • NZ petrol is volatile. 2026 prices are elevated ~35% by an oil shock (95-octane peaked ~NZD $3.62/L on 20 Apr 2026); long-run is ~NZD $2.50/L. Treat as a sensitivity input.
  • Visas. NZ free (Trans-Tasman); Georgia free (365 days); Mexico free (180 days); Canada eTA ~$7; France/Schengen ETIAS €20/adult from late 2026 (under-18s exempt → the child pays nothing).
  • Daily food & living figures are per family of three, including local transport. Travel insurance, storage and an $800/mo unforeseen buffer apply across all scenarios.
