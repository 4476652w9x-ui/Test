A finance practice for Australian wine.

I’m a chartered accountant. Twenty years in senior finance, mostly in professional services, with sell-side M&A across that career. I’m starting a practice focused on Australian wine. Practice

Strategic finance, M&A, wine. Most CAs don’t do M&A. Most M&A people don’t end up in wine. The overlap is small — which is roughly the point of this practice.

Strategic Finance

Chartered Accountant. Twenty years in senior finance. Forecasting, large-scale cost modelling, and the kind of decision support a board actually reads.

M&A

Sell-side M&A in professional services. Pricing a business where the value sits in people, brand, and recurring engagement turns out to be a closer cousin to pricing a winery than to pricing most other things.

Wine

WSET Level 3, in progress. The interest is real, the credentialling is deliberate. Wine is where the practice goes from here.

Offer

Two pieces of work, at no cost. Two engagements, free, for a small number of Australian wine producers selected through this Expression of Interest.

01

A 13-week rolling cashflow model.

Anchored to the vintage cycle, built from your own accounting data, delivered as a dashboard specific to the business. The point is that it gets used after I leave.

02

A top-down cost model.

Cuts by vintage, varietal, and channel. Built from the same ledger data and reconciled back to your P&L and balance sheet — so margin across DTC, cellar door, club, wholesale, and export ties to your accounts, rather than living in a separate spreadsheet.

Both pieces delivered inside the pilot window. Turnaround is short.

Exchange

What I ask in return. Two things. Neither is financial.

Candid feedback

On the work, on the engagement, and on the offer itself. At this stage of the practice, an honest read is worth more than a fee.

A testimonial — only if the work earns it

If you don’t think it earned one, that’s the end of it. No follow-up, no nudge. The offer is not contingent on a testimonial; the request is contingent on you finding the work worth one.

That’s the whole exchange. No retainer behind it, no upsell, no follow-on obligation.

Eligibility

Open to Australian wine producers of any size. Any scale, boutique through mid-tier, anywhere in Australia. Delivered remotely; one or two in-person sessions if it makes sense.

Selection priorities

  • Owner-operated or family-held producers
  • Willing to share working financial data under NDA
  • A clear question or decision the modelling can pressure-test
  • A balance of regions, varietals, and scale across the cohort

Three to five producers in the cohort.

How it runs

From first conversation to delivered models.

  1. 01

    30-minute call

    Confirm fit, scope, and the financial question at the centre of the engagement.

  2. 02

    NDA and data read-in

    NDA signed, working financial data read in. Nothing bespoke is needed from you — the work uses what is already in the ledger.

  3. 03

    Build

    Models built, then walked through with you in a working session. Assumptions tested, questions answered.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    Final models handed over, a short written read on what the numbers show, and a feedback conversation on the work and the offer.

  5. 05

    Testimonial — optional

    Only if you think the work earned one.

Measured in weeks.
