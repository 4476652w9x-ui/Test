Decision 04 · Pricing model

Your agreed daily rate is a margin decision. Most firms set it like a salary calculation.

The agreed daily rate is the single number a professional-services firm touches most often and reasons about least. It is set once, indexed to wages, and quietly carried for years — long after the cost base, the leverage and the recoverability behind it have moved.

Run it the other way. Start from a target margin, layer in raw versus effective cost by FTE band, apply your real recoverability, and the daily rate falls out as an output, not an input.

The maths, one firm

Effective cost / day (Manager)$640
Target margin34.0%
Recoverability91.2%
Implied agreed daily rate$1,065

The firm was charging $920. Not a pricing problem on the surface — a recoverability problem underneath it, hidden by a rate that was never derived from the cost it was meant to cover.

Bookkeepers record the past. Controllers report the present. Pricing the work is a third seat.
